## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a refined, literary-focused visual identity for Teuku Aqiel, an Indonesian Content Writer, Editor, Proofreader, and Scriptwriter. 

The aesthetic channels the prestige and quiet confidence of an independent literary journal, high-end editorial press, and modern cultural essay platform. It balances disciplined minimalism with tactile warmth, avoiding sterile tech conventions in favor of organic, page-like structure. 

### Core Attributes
- **Literary & Contemplative:** Emphasizes long-form legibility, measured pacing, and poetic restraint.
- **Authoritative Craft:** Reflects meticulous editorial standards through sharp typographic hierarchy and structured hairline separators.
- **Warm & Organic:** Subdued paper and sand tones replace stark whites, evoking physical publication sheets, archival bindings, and natural inks.
- **Indonesian Cultural Nuance:** Native UI labeling with natural, sophisticated phrasing (e.g., *Karya Terpilih*, *Tentang*, *Jasa Editorial*, *Hubungi*), maintaining an intellectual yet accessible tone.

## Colors

The color palette is derived from physical archival print: soft alabaster paper, deep carbon ink, slate annotations, and a single muted terracotta accent drawn from classic bookbinder cloth and editor's ink.

### Palette Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#FAF9F6`):** Soft alabaster. Used for full-page canvas background to diminish glare and evoke unbleached paper.
- **Surface Elevation / Cards (`#F2EFE9`):** Soft sand/linen tint. Creates quiet contrast without requiring deep shadows.
- **Surface Elevated Interactive (`#E8E3DA`):** Slightly deeper sand tone for hover states and secondary action badges.
- **Primary Ink / Headings (`#1F2421`):** Deep carbon charcoal. Replaces harsh `#000000` to deliver high editorial legibility with soft optical presence.
- **Muted Ink / Body Copy (`#5C6360`):** Muted slate gray. Tuned for sustained reading across long essays, script samples, and project descriptions.
- **Accent Terracotta (`#C06C54`):** Soft terracotta. Used sparingly for interactive focal points, category pill badges, byline highlights, link text hover, and primary CTAs.
- **Hairline Border (`#E4DFD5`):** 1px subtle divider tone to mimic ruled publishing margins.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects dual operational needs: literary immersion and precise functional navigation.

- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display introduces classic, intellectual grandeur for hero declarations, article titles, and script headers. It holds high contrast strokes and graceful terminals.
- **Reading Body:** Newsreader handles long-form excerpts, portfolio case summaries, and proofreading comparisons, offering exceptional prose flow and rhythm.
- **UI & Interface Metadata:** Plus Jakarta Sans grounds the interface in contemporary clarity. It manages navigation links, metadata counters, tags, timestamps, and button labels in uppercase or tracked lowercase.

## Layout & Spacing

The portfolio employs a deliberate, asymmetrical fluid-grid system structured to emulate editorial column spreads.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column layout with a generous `margin` of 4rem (64px) and `gutter` of 1.5rem (24px). Long-form essays are constrained to a centered 7-to-8 column measure (max-width: 720px) to maintain optimal reading line length.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `margin-tablet` of 2rem (32px) and `gutter-tablet` of 1.25rem (20px). Multi-column case grids compress into balanced 2-column cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile` of 1.25rem (20px) and `gutter-mobile` of 1rem (16px). All content reflows to a clean single column.

### Rhythmic Philosophy
Space is treated as positive compositional breathing room. Vertical distance between distinct sections scales generously (`space-xl` and above) to let each creative domain (Penulisan Naskah, Penyuntingan, Penulisan Artikel) sit in isolation without sensory clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews modern volumetric drop-shadows and neomorphic effects, relying entirely on **tonal layering and low-contrast outlines** to create visual hierarchy.

- **Ground Level:** `#FAF9F6` base page surface.
- **Mid-Tier Elements (Article Cards, Bio Containers, Work Sheets):** Built with `#F2EFE9` surfaces paired with a crisp `1px solid #E4DFD5` border.
- **Active / Hover Elevating:** Rather than floating upward via heavy shadows, hovered containers transition their background to `#EBE6DC` and subtly darken their border to `#D5CFC3` with a smooth 200ms ease curve.
- **Overlays / Menus / Dialogs:** Elevated surfaces employ an ultra-subtle tinted ambient diffuse shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.05)`, preserving the clean two-dimensional nature of paper.

## Shapes

The design system uses a subtle roundedness standard (`2` / 8px base).

- Standard interactive components (buttons, input fields, cards, modal panels) employ a precise `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius. This softens the interface without feeling overly playful or bubbly.
- Category tags, metadata chips, and small pill status indicators employ fully rounded pill radii (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to contrast with architectural rectangular cards.
- Divider lines, table rules, and manuscript pull-quotes use strictly squared, sharp 1px strokes.

## Components

### 1. Buttons (Tombol Tindakan)
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Terracotta (`#C06C54`) background, text in `#FAF9F6`, 8px radius, font `label-lg`. Padding: `12px 24px`. Hover state: `#A85842`. Transition: `background-color 200ms ease`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#1F2421`, text in `#1F2421`. Hover state: `#1F2421` fill with `#FAF9F6` text.
- **Text Link CTA:** Newsreader or Plus Jakarta Sans with a persistent 1px underline in `#C06C54` offset by 4px. On hover, the link color transitions to `#C06C54`.

### 2. Editorial Work Cards (Kartu Portofolio)
- Container background `#F2EFE9`, bordered with 1px `#E4DFD5`, 8px radius.
- Padding: 28px desktop, 20px mobile.
- Features category tag (e.g., *Naskah Film*, *Esai Budaya*, *Penyuntingan Buku*) at the top, followed by the title in `headline-md` (`Playfair Display`), client/publication metadata in `label-md`, and excerpt in `body-sm`.

### 3. Category Tags & Chips (Label Kategori)
- Background `#E8E3DA`, text in `#C06C54`, full pill radius (`9999px`).
- Typography: `label-sm` in all-caps with `0.08em` letter spacing.
- Padding: `4px 12px`.

### 4. Input Fields & Textareas (Formulir Kontak)
- Background `#FAF9F6`, border: `1px solid #D5CFC3`, 8px radius.
- Text: `#1F2421`, placeholder: `#8C9390`.
- Focus ring: `1px solid #C06C54` without aggressive outer glow, keeping focus crisp and minimal.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base box/circle: `18px`, border `1.5px solid #5C6360`, background `#FAF9F6`.
- Checked state: `#C06C54` fill with white checkmark.

### 6. Editorial Comparison Component (Uji Sunting / Before & After)
- A specialized component displaying original vs. proofread drafts.
- Two-column split container with vertical 1px divider in `#E4DFD5`. Left column displays unedited copy with strikethrough notation; right column displays Teuku Aqiel’s polished edit with subtle terracotta inline additions.

### 7. Manuscript Excerpt Drawer (Kutipan Naskah)
- Indented blockquote styled with a 2px left border in `#C06C54`, background transparent or `#F2EFE9`, typography in `quote` (`Newsreader` italic), followed by source attribution in `label-md`.
